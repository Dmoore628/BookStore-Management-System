This course is a direct execution and delivery phase of your Capstone project. It runs on a 2-sprint Scrum cycle across 5 weeks, where Weeks 1 and 3 focus on planning and mid-sprint hour tracking, Weeks 2 and 4 require working software increments and retrospectives, and Week 5 is reserved for demos, slide presentations, and peer evaluations.

### Critical Checkpoints to Manage

**1. Immediate Team Alignment (Unit 1)**

* Confirm all CS491 teammates immediately. If anyone has dropped or is unresponsive, notify the instructor at once to avoid losing 25 points on the communication criteria or getting stalled on merging CS491 documents.
* Harmonize the individual CS491 documents into a single source of truth rather than copying and pasting separate sections without unifying them.

**2. The 2-Week Sprint Cycle Pattern**

* **Odd Weeks (1 & 3):** Focus on updating the planning sheets, assigning task owners, logging non-development time (meetings, architecture planning, environment setup), and recording tracked hours.
* **Even Weeks (2 & 4):** Development freezes for the sprint increment. Every submission requires a fully packaged zip file with both the source code and an executable build, accompanied by the Retrospective table ("What went well", "What didn't", and "Actionable improvements").

**3. Unit 5 Slide Deck Constraints**
The final presentation has an exact 5-slide limit:

* Slide 1: Product Backlog
* Slides 2–3: Sprint 1 Planning and Retrospective
* Slides 4–5: Sprint 2 Planning and Retrospective

### Recommended First Steps for This Week

* Set up a shared workspace (e.g., Google Drive, GitHub repository, and Discord/Slack channel) with your CS491 group members.
* Consolidate your individual CS491 project specifications into the master Project Document.
* Populate the Sprint 1 Planning sheet with granular user stories, set realistic initial estimates, and assign team member names to each backlog item.
* Establish a daily or mid-week cadence for logging hours on the sheet so tracking is not left until Sunday night.
